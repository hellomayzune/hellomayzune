# Hi, I'm [May Zune](https://mayzune.com/) 👋

I am a building scientist with a strong interest in health-environmental data science for a net-zero built environment.

I completed my PhD in Building Technology at the University of Nottingham and have since worked as a researcher at the University of Sheffield, Brunel University London, and the University of Nottingham. I just completed the [Professional Certificate in Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence](https://certificates.emeritus.org/bf971f3d-59da-4d3e-8fc9-629eaf3eebbc?learnersui=true) at Imperial College London. In 2024, I completed a Postgraduate Certificate in Higher Education, and I am a certified Passivhaus Designer.

I recently received the 2026 CIBSE President's Award (Brian Moss Fan Makers + BSER&T) to deliver two independent research projects. I also serve as a reviewer for a number of academic journals, which are documented on my verified ORCID record. I have over five years of postdoctoral experience. My research focuses on decarbonisation, housing inequality, fuel poverty, climate change mitigation and adaptation, and indoor environmental quality, spanning both UK/EU and Southeast Asian contexts. Before entering academia, I spent a decade working on Building Information Modelling (BIM) projects in Singapore.

My mission is to leverage health-environmental data science, building physics, and machine learning to drive equitable decarbonisation, mitigate fuel poverty, and build climate resilience through FAIR principles and open data. I actively contribute to open science by developing transparent, accessible data pipelines and tools for researchers and practitioners in the built environment.

You can find me on [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-0282-2633), [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LmP8B_4AAAAJ&hl=en), [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/May-Zune), [GitHub](https://github.com/hellomayzune), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mayzune/), and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/mayzune.bsky.social).
